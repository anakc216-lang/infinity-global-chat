⚠️ Stay Safe

⚠️ IMPORTANT

Never send money, passwords, OTPs, banking details, or other sensitive information to people you meet in chat.

Be especially careful of:

  • Investment Scams — guaranteed profits, crypto/investment opportunities, requests to deposit money
  • Love & Romance Scams — people building relationships and asking for money, gifts, or investments
  • Phishing Scams — suspicious links asking you to log in, verify accounts, or provide personal info
  • Fake Offers & Giveaways — fake prizes, jobs, rewards requiring payment or personal information
  • Impersonation Scams — people pretending to be friends, companies, government, or celebrities

Never share:

  • Passwords or PINs
  • OTPs (One-Time Passwords)
  • Banking details or card information
  • Other sensitive personal information

Remember: Never send money to someone you only know through chat.

💡 Report suspicious activity immediately by using the Report button on any message.
